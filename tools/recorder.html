Voice recorder — replace the robot placeholder voices with a real human voice
How: click ● Record, say the sound/word, click again to stop, ▶ to check, then ✔ Keep (it downloads a file). When you have a batch, put the downloaded rec_*.webm files in one folder and run node tools/apply_recordings.mjs <folder> — it trims, converts and replaces the placeholders.
Letter sounds: say the PURE sound — "mmm", "sss", "t" — with no extra "uh" ("tuh", "muh" is wrong). Stops (t, p, k, d, b, g, j, ch) are short and clipped. Speak a few words slowly and clearly; natural intonation is fine. Say your own accent consistently (the app is set to American English; keep yours if you prefer — just be consistent).
